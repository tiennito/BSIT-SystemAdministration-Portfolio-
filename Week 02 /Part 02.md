## 2.4 Hardware Justification

The company requires exactly 20 primary computers for 20 employees. Instead of purchasing 20 desktops, six employees are given laptops to support mobility.

A 48-port managed switch provides considerably more capacity than the company currently requires. The unused ports allow the organization to add employees, additional servers, IP phones, security cameras, access points, and other devices without immediately replacing the core switch.

Two wireless access points provide better coverage and distribute wireless clients more effectively than relying on a single access point.

The NAS provides centralized storage while the two external drives allow backup media to be rotated, including keeping one copy disconnected or stored outside the primary server area.
